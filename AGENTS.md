# AI Training Materials Repository

This directory collects materials from different training sessions about using AI in software development. Each subdirectory is an independent project and may have its own instructions, structure, and Git history.

## Contents

- `ai-sdlc-training/` — materials from a previous training session prepared by colleagues for another team.
- `claude-ai-training/` — materials from a separate training session about Claude and agentic workflows, also previously delivered to another team.
- `palo-alto-training/` — materials being prepared for the Palo Alto team, which works primarily in C++.
- `PRESENTATION-STORYTELLING.md` — general guidance for building presentation narratives.

## Working Guidelines

- Treat existing training materials as sources of inspiration and examples, not as templates to copy without adaptation.
- Adapt content, examples, and exercises to the team context documented in the relevant directory.
- Do not mix materials from different training sessions. Keep audience-specific changes in the corresponding subdirectory.
- Before editing a project, check its local `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, and other instructions.
- Keep source materials, audience context, agendas, and final training materials clearly separated.
