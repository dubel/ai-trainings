Interaction modes

Ponytail reduces code; Caveman reduces prose

01

Ponytail

Prefer the smallest correct change, existing platform features, and one focused check.

02

Caveman

Compress communication into direct facts, decisions, commands, and evidence.

Ponytail is an implementation discipline: minimal surface area, no speculative abstractions, no unnecessary dependencies. Caveman is a communication discipline: short, precise, technically complete messages.

Prompt to contract

A task contract removes unsafe assumptions

ProblemExploreConvergeEvidence
ScopeFiles, behavior, and explicit exclusions.
ConstraintsPlatforms, compatibility, security, and style.
AcceptanceCommands, expected results, and proof.
Contrast “fix the parser” with a bounded task contract. A good contract names scope, constraints, acceptance criteria, and evidence. The model can then reason without inventing missing requirements.

A Linux build cannot prove an MSVC claim. A 64-bit test cannot prove 32-bit behavior. Encode repeatable GCC and MSVC workflows as skills, then report evidence for each claimed target.

Evidence contract

Every success claim needs observable proof

Claim

State exactly what now works.

Command

Record the reproducible check.

Observation

Capture output, artifact, or behavior.

Boundary

Name what remains unverified.

Keep proof boundaries honest. Local tests, CI, review, deployment, and live behavior are distinct. A successful build proves compilation. It does not prove production behavior.
